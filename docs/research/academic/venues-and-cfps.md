## arXiv (do this early, regardless)

Post a consolidated preprint to **arXiv `cs.CR`**. It's *not* peer review and
confers little credibility on its own, but it timestamps the work, invites
feedback, and is the natural home for the notes you already have.
**Caveat:** a first `cs.CR` submission needs an **endorsement** or an
academic‑affiliated email — line this up in advance.

## Recommended sequence (given it's Aug 2026)

1. **Now → Sep.** Post the arXiv preprint. Submit the **tool** to **Black Hat
   Europe Arsenal** (confirm deadline) and **OWASP AppSec Days Portugal** (Sep 23)
   — cheap reputation/reach while the real paper is built. These are *tool demos*,
   not the credibility win, but they are honest and visible.
2. **Sep → early Oct.** If **Paper B** (agent‑auth) can be made rigorous quickly,
   target **AIDC 2026 (Sep 25)** and/or **SaTML 2027 (Sep 29)** — the near‑term,
   on‑topic academic‑adjacent shots. A workshop‑length, well‑measured version is
   realistic here.
3. **Oct → Nov.** Build **Paper A** (the measurement study — the defensible
   academic contribution). Aim it at **NDSS 2027 fall (~Nov)** or **IEEE S&P 2027
   (Nov 17)** *if* the labelled validation + CIs are solid; otherwise hold for
   **USENIX Security '27 Cycle 2 (Jan 26 2027)**, which gives more runway and is
   the friendliest top venue for measurement work.
4. **2027.** Fill the calendar with **SecDev 2027, WOOT 2027, AISec 2027,
   ACSAC 2027, LASER** as complementary/fallback, and pursue **Artifact Evaluation
   badges** wherever the target venue offers them.

**Do not** open at a Tier‑1 venue with the current self‑authored benchmark as the
headline — that is the "making it wrong" you want to avoid. Land the real
evaluation first.
